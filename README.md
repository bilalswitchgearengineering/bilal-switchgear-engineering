# Bilal Switchgear Engineering (Pvt) Ltd.

**Official GitHub repository for [bilaleng.com](https://bilaleng.com)**

Established in **1978**, Bilal Switchgear Engineering is a Lahore-based industrial electrical engineering company and one of Pakistan's most trusted names in switchgear manufacturing and power distribution solutions.

---

## 🏭 Who We Are

We are an **ABB-licensed switchgear manufacturer** and **SIEMENS System Integrator** with over 45 years of experience delivering certified electrical engineering solutions across Pakistan.

Our clients include **GEPCO**, **Coca-Cola**, **Marriott Hotels**, **Swat Tunnel Project**, and hundreds of industrial and commercial facilities across the country.

📍 **Location:** Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan
